Install

Add it to Gmail

Do this on a computer in Gmail on the web. The signature then appears on every device that uses Gmail on the web, including Gmail opened in a phone browser.

  1. Copy your signature

    Press "Copy signature" above. If your browser blocks it, the signature gets highlighted instead; press Ctrl+C, or ⌘C on a Mac.

  2. Open Gmail settings

    In Gmail, click the gear icon at top right, then See all settings.

  3. Create a signature

    On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it, for example "Nexority".

  4. Paste it in

    Click in the signature box and paste with Ctrl+V, or ⌘V on a Mac. Don't retype or reformat anything.

  5. Set the defaults

    Under Signature defaults, choose it for "For new emails use". For "On reply/forward use", pick the same one or a Reply-layout signature made here.

  6. Save changes

    Scroll to the very bottom of the page and click Save Changes. Gmail discards the signature if you leave without saving.
